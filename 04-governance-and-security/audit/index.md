# Audit

Audit is the record of what happened on your platform: who did what, to which
object, and when. It underpins security investigations, compliance evidence, and
day-to-day operational debugging. This sub-section describes what Allodium
captures and how to get it into your own systems.

Audit records are append-only. Neither administrators nor Allodium can quietly
edit history — the log is a durable account of activity that you can retain for
as long as your policies require.

Start with [audit logs](01-audit-logs.md) for the event model and export
options.
